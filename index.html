Welcome to Science Through Coding

This is your science book built with code. We will learn one concept at a time, and build the page gradually.

Step 1: Understand what you see on this page.

Physics (Starting Point)

Physics helps us understand how the world works — motion, energy, sound, light, and electricity.

Energy Around Us

Energy is everywhere. Heat from the sun, sound from speakers, light from bulbs, and movement in vehicles are all forms of energy.

Sound Basics

Sound is vibration. It travels through air, water, and solids.

Light Basics

Light helps us see. It travels very fast and can reflect from surfaces.

Electricity Basics

Electricity powers our homes, devices, and machines.

Electric Circuits

A circuit is a complete path for electricity to flow.

Chemistry (Coming Next)

After physics, we will learn matter, atoms, reactions, and materials.

Computer Engineering (Coming Next)
